The SM-1 leak test's library and questions (Story 3.13) in this folder

library/R.txt, HA.txt, NB.txt and X.txt are invented game content, written for this test in Portuguese. They quote no
published game, book or website, and every name, rule and number in them is made up:

- R.txt: the rulebook of the invented system "Vale de Ferro", attached to that system;
- HA.txt: campaign "Casa"'s house rules. Each one contradicts a rule of R.txt and carries its own marker word
  (corvoalto, pedrafunda, ventolargo, luzerna, brasaviva, cinzarrubra), so an answer that repeats a house rule
  can be told by that word;
- NB.txt: campaign "Livro"'s session notes, by the book: they change no rule;
- X.txt: the rulebook of a second invented system, "Mar de Cinzas", attached to that system.

The pipeline turns each file into a text-only PDF (gmshield_ci/sm2.text_pdf): a line holding only "---" starts a new
page. questions.json holds the 40 questions asked in both campaigns; the leak gate (gmshield_ci/leak.py, part A)
and the real-model run (part B) share them.
