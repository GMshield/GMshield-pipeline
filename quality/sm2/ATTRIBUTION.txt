The SM-2 question set (Story 2.9) in this folder

questions.json holds 50 questions about the System Reference Document 5.2.1, 25 in Portuguese and 25 in English,
written for these checks. Each question carries short answer phrases quoted from the SRD 5.2.1 (at most 15 words)
and the pages they appear on; `python3 -m gmshield_ci sm2` looks for them in the passages an answer cites.
